Suggestions to get started

Import from LinkedIn

LinkedIn blocks automated access and their terms forbid it, so nothing here talks to linkedin.com. Paste content you already have and it gets parsed locally: a job alert email, the copied text of a search results page, or one job URL per line (optionally url | Title | Company | Location).

Imported postings rarely carry a description, so they score on title, company, and location alone. Where the company runs its own Greenhouse, Lever, or Ashby board, add it above instead — the agent can poll that properly.

Paste any job

From Google Jobs, Rozee.pk, Mustakbil, a company page, or an email — anything without a public API. Nothing is fetched; what you paste is what gets scored and tailored.

Most reliable is a few label lines then the description. Separate several jobs with a line of ---:

Title: Senior Backend Engineer
Company: Systems Ltd
Location: Lahore, Pakistan
URL: https://www.rozee.pk/job/12345
